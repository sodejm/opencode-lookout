# Code of conduct

Help make this project useful and welcoming. Treat people with respect regardless
of background, identity, experience, or technical ability. Critique ideas and code,
not people. Accept constructive feedback and acknowledge mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized content, personal attacks,
and publishing someone else's private information are not acceptable. Do not use
project channels for spam or repeated unwanted contact.

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces where
someone represents the project. The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content,
warn participants, restrict participation, or ban accounts according to severity
and repeated behavior. Maintainers are also bound by this policy.

Report concerns to [@sodejm](https://github.com/sodejm). For sensitive incidents,
request a private contact method in a minimal issue without naming affected people
or describing the incident publicly. Share details only after a private channel
is established. The maintainer will limit disclosure to what is needed to address
the concern. No response-time guarantee is made.
